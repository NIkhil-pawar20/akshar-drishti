What happens to your page

Upload a scanned government order, notice or certificate. The system reads it, checks it and protects it in one step.

1 · ReadFinds titles, paragraphs and tables, then reads them in order with IndicOCR.
2 · VerifyLooks for the officer's signature and the office stamp.
3 · ExtractPulls out dates, survey numbers, amounts, PIN codes and more, including ०–९ digits.
4 · Protect & storeHides Aadhaar and PAN numbers and saves a searchable record.
